# Quantitative Risk Management: VaR & CVaR

Systematic measurement of portfolio tail risks and downside exposures.

---

## 1. VaR & Expected Shortfall (CVaR)

- **Value at Risk ($VaR_\alpha$):** Max loss at confidence level $\alpha$.
- **Expected Shortfall ($CVaR_\alpha$):** Expected loss given loss exceeds VaR (coherent risk measure).

---

## 2. Performance Metrics

Sharpe Ratio, Sortino Ratio, Calmar Ratio, and Maximum Drawdown.
