# Algorithmic Execution: TWAP, VWAP & Almgren-Chriss

Slicing large institutional orders to minimize market impact and timing risk.

---

## 1. TWAP & VWAP Strategies

- **TWAP:** Equal slice sizing $V_i = V_{\text{total}} / N$ across time intervals.
- **VWAP:** Slicing proportional to historical intraday volume profiles.

---

## 2. Almgren-Chriss Model

Balances **Market Impact** (temporary & permanent) against **Timing Risk** (volatility variance).
