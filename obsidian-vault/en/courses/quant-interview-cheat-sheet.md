Rapid reference cheat sheet for quant trading interview prep.

## 1. Key Probability Formulas

- **Geometric $Geom(p)$:** $E[X] = 1/p$.
- **Coupon Collector:** $E[X] \approx N \ln N + \gamma N$.
- **Dice stopping adjustment:** $E[\text{stopping value}] \approx K + 5/3$.
- **Kelly Criterion:** $f^* = \frac{pb-q}{b}$.
- **Put-Call Parity:** $C - P = S - K e^{-rT}$.
