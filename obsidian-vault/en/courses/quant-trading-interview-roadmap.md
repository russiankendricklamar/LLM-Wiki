# 30-Day Quant Trading Interview Preparation Roadmap

Step-by-step 30-day intensive interview preparation guide for Jane Street, Optiver, SIG, Flow Traders, and Citadel Securities.

---

## Weekly Roadmap

- **Days 1–7:** Mental math speed training & Probability distributions.
- **Days 8–15:** Logic brainteasers & out-loud narration practice.
- **Days 16–22:** Dice games, Kelly Criterion & Optimal stopping.
- **Days 23–27:** Fermi estimation & 90% confidence interval calibration.
- **Days 28–30:** Market Making simulation & Mock interviews.
