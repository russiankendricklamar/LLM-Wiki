# Kelly Criterion & Optimal Stopping Theory

Maximizing capital growth while controlling risk and identifying optimal stopping points.

---

## 1. Kelly Criterion

Optimal wager fraction $f^*$ maximizing logarithmic wealth growth $E[\ln(W)]$:
$$f^* = \frac{p(b+1) - 1}{b} = p - \frac{q}{b}$$

---

## 2. Optimal Stopping & Secretary Problem

Reject the first $N / e \approx 37\%$ candidates, then select the first candidate better than all previous ones.
